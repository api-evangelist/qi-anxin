Overview In early July 2026 we observed a Go-based botnet pushing bot samples onto the internet at scale. It folds scanning, exploitation, and credential/AI-service intelligence harvesting into a single autonomous platform. Because its controller calls itself n4d mesh controller in the source, we named it NadMesh .
